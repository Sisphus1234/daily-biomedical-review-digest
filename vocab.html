senator

词频排名 #2915 · 真题出现 26 次

参议员；古罗马元老院议员

hypothesis //-əsəs//

词频排名 #2923 · 真题出现 26 次

假设，假说；前提

英文释义

  • nounUsed loosely, a tentative conjecture explaining an observation, phenomenon or scientific problem that can be tested by further observation, investigation and/or experimentation. As a scientific term of art, see the attached quotation. Compare to theory, and quotation given there.
  • noun(general) An assumption taken to be true for the purpose of argument or investigation.
  • noun(grammar) The antecedent of a conditional statement.
考点提示复数 hypotheses。常考动词形式 hypothesize。与 theory（理论）、assumption（假定）辨析：hypothesis 需验证。

revive //ɹɪˈvaɪv//

词频排名 #2924 · 真题出现 26 次

复苏，复兴；恢复；重新唤起

英文释义

  • verbTo return to life; to become reanimated or reinvigorated.
  • verbTo return to life; to cause to recover life or strength; to cause to live anew.例：Her grandmother refused to be revived if she lost consciousness.
  • verbTo recover from a state of oblivion, obscurity, neglect, or depression.例：Classical learning revived in the fifteenth century.
考点提示re（再）+ vive（生命）→ 重新有生命。常考 revive the economy/tradition。名词 revival 也常考。

prey //pɹeɪ//

词频排名 #2942 · 真题出现 26 次

猎物；捕食；受害者

英文释义

  • nounAnything, as goods, etc., taken or got by violence; anything taken by force from an enemy in war
  • nounThat which is or may be seized by animals or birds to be devoured; hence, a person given up as a victim.
  • nounA living thing that is eaten by another living thing.例：The rabbit was eaten by the coyote, so the rabbit is the coyote's prey.
考点提示常考搭配 prey on/upon（捕食；折磨）。熟词僻义：be prey to（成为…的受害者）。与 pray（祈祷）拼写易混。

今日学习建议

今日词汇多涉及政治、医学、学术等考研高频话题，注意区分英式与美式拼写（enrol/realise）。重点掌握熟词僻义（如 realise 表“实现”、prey 表“受害者”）及固定搭配（cater to, incorporate into）。建议结合真题例句记忆，并复习同根词（如 senator-senior, revive-vital）。
