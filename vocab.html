enterprise //ˈɛntɚˌpɹaɪz//

词频排名 #2171 · 真题出现 49 次

企业；事业；进取心（熟词僻义：艰巨复杂的计划）

英文释义

  • nounA company, business, organization, or other purposeful endeavor.例：A micro-enterprise is defined as a business having 5 or fewer employees and a low seed capital.
  • nounAn undertaking, venture, or project, especially a daring and courageous one.例：Biosphere 2 was a scientific enterprise aimed at the exploration of the complex web of interactions within life systems.
  • nounA willingness to undertake new or risky projects; energy and initiative.例：He has shown great enterprise throughout his early career.
考点提示enter（进入）+prise（抓住），有进取之意。常考：private enterprise（私营企业），spirit of enterprise（进取精神）。
