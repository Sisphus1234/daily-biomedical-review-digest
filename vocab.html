neighborhood //ˈneɪbə.hʊd//

词频排名 #2076 · 真题出现 54 次

小区、社区；邻近地区；附近

英文释义

  • nounThe quality of being a neighbor, of living nearby, next to each-other; proximity.例：Our neighborhood was our only reason to exchange hollow greetings.
  • nounClose proximity, nearby area; particularly, close proximity to one's home.例：He lives in my neighborhood.
  • nounThe inhabitants of a residential area.例：The fire alarmed the neighborhood.
考点提示注意熟词僻义：可指“邻近地区”或“大约”（in the neighborhood of）。常考搭配：neighborhood watch（社区联防）。

词频排名 #2085 · 真题出现 53 次

业余爱好者；外行；业余的

英文释义

  • nounA lover of something.
  • nounA person attached to a particular pursuit, study, or science as to music or painting; especially one who cultivates any study or art, from taste or attachment, without pursuing it professionally.例：The contest is only open to amateurs.
