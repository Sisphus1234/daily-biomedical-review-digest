## 1. neighborhood  //ˈneɪbə.hʊd//

**词频排名 #2076 · 真题出现 54 次** ｜ 分类：空间地点

**其他拼写：** neighbourhood

**中文释义：** 小区、社区；邻近地区；附近

**英文释义：**

- *noun* The quality of being a neighbor, of living nearby, next to each-other; proximity.
  - 例：_Our neighborhood was our only reason to exchange hollow greetings._
- *noun* Close proximity, nearby area; particularly, close proximity to one's home.
  - 例：_He lives in my neighborhood._
- *noun* The inhabitants of a residential area.
  - 例：_The fire alarmed the neighborhood._

**考点提示：** 注意熟词僻义：可指“邻近地区”或“大约”（in the neighborhood of）。常考搭配：neighborhood watch（社区联防）。

## 2. amateur  //ˈæ.mə.tə//

**词频排名 #2085 · 真题出现 53 次** ｜ 分类：运动娱乐

**中文释义：** 业余爱好者；外行；业余的

**英文释义：**

- *noun* A lover of something.
- *noun* A person attached to a particular pursuit, study, or science as to music or painting; especially one who cultivates any study or art, from taste or attachment, without pursuing it professionally.
  - 例：_The contest is only open to amateurs._
- *noun* Someone who is unqualified or insufficiently skillful.
  - 例：_The entire thing was built by some amateurs with screwdrivers and plywood._

**考点提示：** 词根amat-（爱），如amateur意为“出于爱好而非专业”。常与professional（专业）对比，真题中常考“业余”与“专业”的差异。

**中文释义：** 几乎不；仅仅，勉强

**英文释义：**

- *adverb* (degree) By a small margin.
- *adverb* (degree) Almost not at all.
