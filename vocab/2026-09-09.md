## 6. ambiguous  //æmˈbɪɡjuəs//

**词频排名 #2657 · 真题出现 33 次** ｜ 分类：抽象概念

**中文释义：** 模棱两可的；含糊不清的；引起歧义的

**英文释义：**

- *adjective* Open to multiple interpretations.
  - 例：_The politician was criticized for his ambiguous statements and lack of precision._
- *adjective* Vague and unclear.
  - 例：_He gave an ambiguous answer._
- *adjective* (of persons) Hesitant; uncertain; not taking sides.

**考点提示：** 词根ambi（两边）+guous→两边都行→模棱两可。易混词：ambivalent（矛盾心理）。真题常考“ambiguous language”歧义语言。

**英文释义：**

**中文释义：** 逐渐削弱；暗中破坏；侵蚀…的基础

**英文释义：**

- *verb* To weaken or work against; to hinder, sabotage.

## 12. verbal  //ˈvɜː.bəl//

**英文释义：**

- *noun* (grammar) A verb form which does not function as a predicate, or a word derived from a verb. In English, infinitives, participles and gerunds are verbals.

**考点提示：** 词根verb（词）+al→动词的。固定搭配“verbal communication”言语交流。易混词：oral（口头的）。
