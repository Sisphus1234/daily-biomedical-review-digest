**词频排名 #1312 · 真题出现 110 次** ｜ 分类：抽象概念

**中文释义：** 上下文；背景，环境；（事件发生的）来龙去脉

**英文释义：**

- *noun* The surroundings, circumstances, environment, background or settings that determine, specify, or clarify the meaning of an event or other occurrence.
  - 例：_In what context did your attack on him happen? - We had a pretty tense relationship at the time, and when he insulted me I snapped._
- *noun* The text in which a word or passage appears and which helps ascertain its meaning.
  - 例：_Without any context, I can't tell you if the "dish" refers to the food, or the thing you eat it on._

**考点提示：** 词根：con-（一起）+text（编织），即“编织在一起”。常考：in the context of（在……背景下）。注意“环境”义项。
