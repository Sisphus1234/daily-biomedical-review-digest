## 6. hypothesis  //-əsəs//

**词频排名 #2923 · 真题出现 26 次** ｜ 分类：抽象概念

**中文释义：** 假设，假说；前提

**英文释义：**

- *noun* Used loosely, a tentative conjecture explaining an observation, phenomenon or scientific problem that can be tested by further observation, investigation and/or experimentation. As a scientific term of art, see the attached quotation. Compare to theory, and quotation given there.
- *noun* (general) An assumption taken to be true for the purpose of argument or investigation.
- *noun* (grammar) The antecedent of a conditional statement.

**考点提示：** 复数 hypotheses。常考动词形式 hypothesize。与 theory（理论）、assumption（假定）辨析：hypothesis 需验证。

## 7. revive  //ɹɪˈvaɪv//

**词频排名 #2924 · 真题出现 26 次** ｜ 分类：动作行为

**中文释义：** 复苏，复兴；恢复；重新唤起

**英文释义：**

- *verb* To return to life; to become reanimated or reinvigorated.
- *verb* To return to life; to cause to recover life or strength; to cause to live anew.
  - 例：_Her grandmother refused to be revived if she lost consciousness._
- *verb* To recover from a state of oblivion, obscurity, neglect, or depression.
  - 例：_Classical learning revived in the fifteenth century._

**考点提示：** re（再）+ vive（生命）→ 重新有生命。常考 revive the economy/tradition。名词 revival 也常考。

## 14. incorporate  //ɪŋˈkɔɹpɚe(ɪ)t//

**词频排名 #2940 · 真题出现 26 次** ｜ 分类：动作行为

**中文释义：** 合并，纳入；包含；组成公司

**英文释义：**

- *verb* To include (something) as a part.
  - 例：_The design of his house incorporates a spiral staircase._
- *verb* To mix (something in) as an ingredient; to blend
  - 例：_Incorporate air into the mixture._

## 15. prey  //pɹeɪ//

**词频排名 #2942 · 真题出现 26 次** ｜ 分类：动作行为

**中文释义：** 猎物；捕食；受害者

**英文释义：**

- *noun* Anything, as goods, etc., taken or got by violence; anything taken by force from an enemy in war
- *noun* That which is or may be seized by animals or birds to be devoured; hence, a person given up as a victim.
- *noun* A living thing that is eaten by another living thing.
  - 例：_The rabbit was eaten by the coyote, so the rabbit is the coyote's prey._

**考点提示：** 常考搭配 prey on/upon（捕食；折磨）。熟词僻义：be prey to（成为…的受害者）。与 pray（祈祷）拼写易混。
