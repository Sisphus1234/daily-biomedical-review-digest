## 1. hug  //hʌɡ//

**词频排名 #3695 · 真题出现 13 次** ｜ 分类：动作行为

**中文释义：** 拥抱；紧抱，紧靠（熟词僻义：紧靠，如船只紧靠海岸）

**英文释义：**

- *noun* A close embrace, especially when charged with such an emotion as represented by: affection, joy, relief, lust, anger, agression, compassion, and the like, as opposed to being characterized by formality, equivocation or ambivalence (a half-embrace or "little hug").

- *verb* To crouch; huddle as with cold.

**考点提示：** 真题常考僻义：hug the coast/shore（紧靠海岸航行）。记忆：hug 本义“拥抱”，引申为“紧挨着”。
