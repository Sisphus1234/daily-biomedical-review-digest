## 2. enterprise  //ˈɛntɚˌpɹaɪz//

**词频排名 #2171 · 真题出现 49 次** ｜ 分类：商业经济

**其他拼写：** enterprize

**中文释义：** 企业；事业；进取心（熟词僻义：艰巨复杂的计划）

**英文释义：**

- *noun* A company, business, organization, or other purposeful endeavor.
  - 例：_A micro-enterprise is defined as a business having 5 or fewer employees and a low seed capital._
- *noun* An undertaking, venture, or project, especially a daring and courageous one.
  - 例：_Biosphere 2 was a scientific enterprise aimed at the exploration of the complex web of interactions within life systems._
- *noun* A willingness to undertake new or risky projects; energy and initiative.
  - 例：_He has shown great enterprise throughout his early career._

**考点提示：** enter（进入）+prise（抓住），有进取之意。常考：private enterprise（私营企业），spirit of enterprise（进取精神）。
