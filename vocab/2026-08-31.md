## 1. exceptional  //ɪkˈsɛpʃənəl//

**词频排名 #1878 · 真题出现 63 次** ｜ 分类：抽象概念

**中文释义：** 例外的；异常的；杰出的，优秀的

**英文释义：**

- *noun* An exception, or something having an exceptional value
- *adjective* Forming an exception; not ordinary; uncommon; rare.
  - 例：_What an exceptional flower!_
- *adjective* Better than the average; superior due to exception or rarity.
  - 例：_The quality of the beer was exceptional._

**考点提示：** 词根cept=拿，ex-向外，拿出来的→例外的。常考义项：杰出的（exceptional talent）。
