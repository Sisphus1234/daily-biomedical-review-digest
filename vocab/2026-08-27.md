## 1. write  //ɹaɪt//

**词频排名 #62 · 真题出现 2693 次** ｜ 分类：动作行为

**中文释义：** 写，写作；写信；编写；记录；[僻义] 承购，认购（股票等）

**英文释义：**

- *noun* The act or style of writing.
- *noun* The operation of storing data, as in memory or onto disk.
  - 例：_How many writes per second can this hard disk handle?_
- *verb* To form letters, words or symbols on a surface in order to communicate.
  - 例：_The pupil wrote his name on the paper._

**考点提示：** 注意 write off 表示‘注销、取消’，write up 表示‘补写、报道’。考研阅读中常出现 write about/on 等搭配。
