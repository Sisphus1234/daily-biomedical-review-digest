**中文释义：** 破坏性的；毁灭性的

**英文释义：**

- *adjective* Causing destruction; damaging.
- *adjective* Causing breakdown or disassembly.
  - 例：_Catabolism is a destructive metabolism that involves the breakdown of molecules and release of energy._
- *adjective* Lossy; causing irreversible change.
