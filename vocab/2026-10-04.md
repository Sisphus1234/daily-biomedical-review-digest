## 7. transit  //ˈtɹæn.sɪt//

**词频排名 #3788 · 真题出现 13 次** ｜ 分类：交通旅行

**中文释义：** 运输，运送；通过（熟词僻义：in transit 在运输中）

**英文释义：**

- *noun* The act of passing over, across, or through something.
- *noun* The conveyance of people or goods from one place to another, especially on a public transportation system; the vehicles used for such conveyance.
  - 例：_the transit of goods through a country_
- *noun* The passage of a celestial body across the observer's meridian, or across the disk of a larger celestial body.

**考点提示：** 词根trans（穿过）+it（走）。常考搭配：mass transit（公共交通），in transit（在途中）。
